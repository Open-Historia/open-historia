# The player's focus outranks the author's world share

A scenario author can require a minimum share of each jump to be World events (the world's share, 35% by default), and the player can choose a Player focus that sets a minimum share of Player events. When the two add up to more than 100%, the player's focus wins and the world's share shrinks to what is left: World first 25% leaves the author's 35% intact, but Spotlight 75% cuts it to 25%. The world's share exists so the rest of the world never goes silent, and a quarter of every jump still does that. It was never meant to overrule how much a player wants to follow their own country.

## Considered Options

- **The author's share wins and caps the player's choice.** Rejected. A player who picked Spotlight would get a setting that quietly does nothing in scenarios with a high world share, with no way to see why.
- **Neither wins; both are scaled down in proportion.** Rejected. It honours neither choice, and the resulting numbers are hard to explain in a settings panel.

## Consequences

- An author who wants the world to stay prominent should raise the world's share and write world-facing guidance. A player on Focused or Spotlight can still push the world's share below the author's number, down to 25%.
- Player focus only counts what the player actually has going on. In a quiet stretch the World fills the jump up to its usual event count, so a high focus never invents Player events.
