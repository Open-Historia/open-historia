The music of Open Historia

All seven pieces are by Scott Buckley (https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/) and are
released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
(CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the files are the composer's own recordings, re-encoded at a lower
bitrate (MP3, about half the size together) so that the game is smaller to
download. Nothing else about them is changed: every piece is whole, at its
own length. Each file names its piece, its composer and this licence in its
tags.

passage-of-time.mp3
    "Passage Of Time" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/passage-of-time/
    Plays on the menu, as far as its build, and from its build on while a
    game opens.

clear-skies.mp3
    "Clear Skies" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/clear-skies/
    Plays on the menu, in turn with Passage Of Time, and from its own build
    on while a game opened under it loads.

frankincense-and-myrrh.mp3
    "Frankincense and Myrrh" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/frankincense-and-myrrh/
    Plays in a game, in the background.

honour-among-thieves.mp3
    "Honour Among Thieves" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/honour-among-thieves/
    Plays in a game, in the background.

discovery.mp3
    "Discovery" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/discovery/
    Plays in a game, in the background.

legionnaire.mp3
    "Legionnaire (2022 Remaster)" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/legionnaire/
    Plays in the war room and while a battlefield is shown.

vanguard.mp3
    "Vanguard" by Scott Buckley
    https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/vanguard/
    Plays in the war room and while a battlefield is shown.
